As a Groundwater Technician with the Idaho Department of Water Resources, I gained hands-on experience conducting groundwater monitoring and water-quality sampling at wells across the entire state of Idaho. My work included collecting water samples for laboratory analysis, measuring depth to groundwater, and recording field parameters such as pH, conductivity, and dissolved oxygen. I also coordinated sampling visits with private well owners, which strengthened my communication skills and ability to manage fieldwork independently and professionally. Through this position, I developed practical experience with environmental sampling procedures, field instrumentation, accurate data collection and documentation, and working safely in a variety of field conditions. This experience strengthened my interest in hydrogeology and environmental science and gave me a practical understanding of how groundwater data are collected and used to evaluate water resources.
