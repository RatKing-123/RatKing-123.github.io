## Education

Olivia graduated from Vanderbilt University with a B.S. in Earth & Environmental Sciences and minors in Digital Fabrication and Art. She has gained a strong foundation in environmental science and sustainability principles through hands-on fieldwork experiences and rigorous coursework. 

## Technical Skills & Experience

Olivia has experience with Fusion360 and Fused Filament Fabrication (FFF). She has gained laboratory and field skills through her work as a Groundwater Technician with the Idaho Department of Water Resources and stream restoration research with Environment Canterbury.

### Environmental Fieldwork:
Water-quality sampling, groundwater well measurements, depth-to-water, pH, conductivity, dissolved oxygen, water chemistry, kick-net sampling of macroinvertebrates
### Laboratory & Analysis: 
XRF geochemical analysis, organic carbon analysis, grain-size analysis, NMDS ordination
### Software & Design: 
Fusion 360 and AutoCAD 3D modeling, ArcGIS, Microsoft Office, Python &  Java programming
### Documentation & Safety: 
Technical report writing, dimensioned drawings, laboratory and fieldwork experience, compliance with safety procedures
